    https://teegardenmath.github.io/x-queue/

How it works:
- Be logged into X in the same browser.
- Each card has Reply / Like / Repost buttons. Reply opens X's compose box already addressed to that post. Like and Repost open a small window with one confirming click.
- Hit Done or Skip when you've dealt with a card and it drops out of "Up next". The Handled tab has a "Put back" button if you change your mind. (That list is remembered by your browser, so stick to one browser if you can.)
- Keyboard: j / k move between posts, r reply, l like, p repost, o open on X, d done, s skip.
- Long posts have a "Show more" toggle, and non-English posts show an English translation with "Show original".
